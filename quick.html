Case study

ExamFlow.in: Meta Ads for a UPSC study product

I built the store, the product and the offer myself, then tested which creative angle would get UPSC aspirants to buy.

1

Build

A ready-made UPSC study system on Shopify, priced ₹259–₹329. Store, pages, copy and pricing all done myself.

2

Test

4 creator-led UGC ads, each a different angle, in one sales campaign with the same audience.

3

Learn

The "night routine" angle won with 1.28% CTR, over 2× the rest, and drove the first sale.

4

Next

Visitors reached the page but few added to cart, so the offer and product page are the next test.
